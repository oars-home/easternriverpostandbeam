Traditional Craft

The Art of Post & Beam

Every Eastern River building is fabricated using centuries-old heavy timber framing techniques, precision mortise and tenon joinery, and sustainably sourced woods.

Mortise & Tenon Joinery

Hand-cut interlocking mortises, tenons, and hardwood trunnel pegs secure each joint without relying on nails or modern fasteners.

Native Eastern Timbers

Carefully selected eastern white pine, hemlock, and red oak timbers chosen for structural integrity, grain beauty, and thermal mass.

Custom Engineering

Each frame layout is custom designed to maximize natural daylight, cathedral ceilings, open floor plans, and long-term energy efficiency.

Community Raisings

The culmination of shop fabrication comes together on raising day, assembling bents and raising the frame with precision and shared pride.

About Us

Built with Pride in Vermont

Eastern River Post and Beam specializes in designing and building custom timber frame homes, barns, outbuildings, and structural additions. Rooted in Vermont's rich tradition of woodworking, we work closely with clients to create architectural spaces that celebrate natural wood, soaring volumes, and structural strength.

From initial conceptual sketches through joinery cutting in our shop and final frame erection, we take pride in personal attention and timeless craftsmanship on every project.

  • Individualized design tailored to your site and lifestyle
  • Meticulous mortise and tenon joinery cut to exact tolerances
  • Integration with high-performance insulation & structural enclosures
